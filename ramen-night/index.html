🚗 How much does distance add?

These are budget cushions, not app quotes. The page assumes a rough town-based trip distance from Ringwood (not your home). If you see the actual distance in DoorDash or Uber Eats, select a restaurant and replace its assumed mileage below.

Distance budgeting estimate will appear here.

This is our customizable planning assumption, not a DoorDash or Uber per-mile rate. Actual fees can be zero or higher. Do not add this buffer to checkout totals if the app already includes delivery fees and tip.
